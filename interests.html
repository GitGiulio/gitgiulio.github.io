Personal interests

Away from software, I enjoy sport, chess and following developments in space exploration.

Ice skating

Bouldering

Since moving to Denmark, I've taken up bouldering in place of short-track skating. I especially enjoy climbing with friends.

Chess

Rocket science & space

I'm fascinated by propulsion systems, orbital mechanics and spacecraft design. I enjoy learning how rockets work and keeping up with developments in space exploration.
